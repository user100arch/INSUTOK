The Problem

Why Content Creators
Fall Through the Cracks

Traditional insurance and social protection laws don't recognize content creators. When income stops, there's no safety net.

⚡

Algorithm Changes

Platform algorithms can demonetize or reduce reach overnight, wiping out months of income with zero recourse or compensation.

🏥

Illness & Health

Unlike salaried employees, content creators receive zero sick pay. A two-week illness can mean zero income and mounting bills.

🔗

Brand Deal Loss

Sponsorships and brand partnerships can end abruptly due to company budget cuts, shifting trends, or contract disputes.

🚫

Account Deactivation

Platform bans — even temporary ones — eliminate all income instantly with limited appeals processes and no compensation.

📉

Audience Decline

Viral content fades. Engagement drops. Income tied to views and subscriptions can halve in weeks without any wrongdoing.

⚖️

No Legal Protection

ILO, KIPPRA and Fairwork Kenya confirm: gig workers including content creators are excluded from social protection frameworks.

Actuarial Science

The Math Behind
Fair Pricing

Built on peer-reviewed actuarial models adapted for the realities of digital creator income in Kenya.

📊 Claim Frequency Model

Interruptions per year follow a Poisson distribution — ideal for counting rare, independent events over time.

P(N=k) = λk · e-λ / k!
// λ = avg. interruptions/year ≈ 2.0

Survey data from Kenyan creators shows an average of λ = 2.0 interruptions per year, varying by platform and audience size.

📈 Claim Severity Model

Monetary loss per interruption follows a Lognormal distribution — suited to right-skewed income with a zero lower bound.

X' = I × R × D
// I=income, R=ratio, D=days
f(x) = 1/(x'σ√2π)·exp[−(lnx'−μ)²/2σ²]

X' captures the actual monetary loss incorporating both the income replacement ratio and interruption duration.

💰 Premium Calculation

Final premium combines expected loss with actuarial loadings for admin, profit margin, and adverse selection risk.

E[L] = λ × E[X']
Premium = E[L] × (1 + θ)
// θ = loading factor (15–35%)

Example: 2 interruptions/yr × KSh 15,000 severity × 1.20 loading = KSh 36,000/yr

🛡️ Moral Hazard Controls

Multiple mechanisms prevent fraud and reduce moral hazard, keeping premiums fair for the entire pool.

Waiting period: 7–30 days
Deductible: 10–20% co-pay
Payouts tied to: views + engagement

How It Works

Claim in 5 Simple
Steps

Step 01

Interruption Occurs

Your income stops — due to illness, platform action, brand deal cancellation, or other covered event. INSUTOK detects anomalies automatically via API monitoring and sends you a WhatsApp alert.

Step 02

Waiting Period Begins

Your chosen waiting period (7–30 days) starts. This filters minor, self-resolving interruptions and keeps premiums affordable for the entire policyholder pool.

Step 03

Submit Your Claim

Open the INSUTOK app or this website and tap "File Claim." Upload documentation — platform logs, medical certificates, or brand deal correspondence. Takes under 2 minutes.

Step 04

Automated Verification

Our system cross-references your platform data, income history, and submitted documents. Straightforward claims are approved within 48 hours. You receive email and WhatsApp updates throughout.

Step 05

Payment Released

Your income replacement payment is disbursed directly to your M-PESA or bank account — up to 60% of average daily income for each covered day, up to your plan limit.
